About this practice test

Questions are drawn in the same proportions as the official Ohio State Test (OST) Algebra I blueprint's three reporting categories: about 37% Number, Quantities & Equations (official range 33–41%), 43% Functions (official range 41–50%), and 20% Statistics (official range 18–22%). Every question is freely regenerated with new numbers, so retaking a test — even at the same length — rarely repeats a question you've already seen this session.

Item types mirror the released Ohio Algebra I test items: multiple choice (including choosing the right box plot), select-all-that-apply, typed numeric/fraction/coordinate entry, fill-in tables (including two-way tables with several blanks), matching grids, dropdown menus inside a sentence, two-part Part A / Part B items, click-to-select "hot text," graphs, box plots, histograms, and dot plots to read, and an interactive click-to-plot coordinate grid.

The test screen is laid out like the real one, with the same tools across the top: Back / Next / Pause, a Hundreds Table, a Multiplication Table, the official high school Formulas reference sheet, the Ohio Desmos testing calculator (the same one allowed on the whole test — it opens in its own window), Notes for each question, a Line Reader, and Zoom. Use the Items menu to jump to any question, the ≡ menu on each question to Mark for Review, and the ✕ next to a choice to cross it out.

Need a nudge? Each question offers up to two optional hints — a strategy tip, then a worked first step. Taking a hint caps that question's credit at half a point (first hint) or a quarter point (second hint) out of 1, similar to how a partial-credit accommodation works, so it's there if you're stuck but it isn't free.

The performance level on your results (Limited / Basic / Proficient / Accomplished / Advanced) is an approximation for practice purposes only, based on your percent-correct score. It's modeled on Ohio's five real reporting levels and their published Algebra I cut scores — Basic at 682, Proficient at 700, Accomplished at 725, and Advanced at 754 on the state's actual 618–814 scaled-score range for this test — but it is not an official scaled score, which the state computes from a fixed operational form using item-response-theory calibration.

This tool is independent, unofficial practice material and is not produced, reviewed, or endorsed by the Ohio Department of Education.
